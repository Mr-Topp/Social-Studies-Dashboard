Teacher dashboard · one-time setup

Turn on grade changes and class-list editing

This adds a small helper script to your school Google account. Your turn-in script and your Sheet stay exactly as they are. About 3 minutes. You don't need to type your teacher code anywhere.

Do this on a computer, not your phone. Google's script editor is hard to use on a phone.

  1. Copy the script.
  2. Signed in to your school Google account, open script.google.com and start a New project.
  3. Delete everything in the editor, paste, and click Save (the disk icon). Name the project Teacher Tools.
  4. Click Deploy (top right) > New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and pick Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  5. Google asks for permission. Click Authorize access, pick your school account, then Allow. (If you see "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced > Go to Teacher Tools. It's your own script.)
  6. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec) and send it to Claude. Claude connects it to your dashboard.

Anyone using the link still needs your teacher code, so it's safe to keep in the dashboard.
