Whose Normal?

Malachi Ray Rempen is an American cartoonist who moved to Europe and has traveled all over the world. His comic Itchy Feet is about what happens when one culture’s “normal” bumps into another’s. You’ll read 5 of his short strips. Some of them are about how America looks to the rest of the world.

Culture shockEthnocentrismCultural relativismNorms

What to do:

  • Read 5 short comic strips, one at a time.
  • Answer 2 checks for each strip. On 2 strips, write a quick “look closer” note first.
  • Finish with 2–3 sentences, then press Turn in answers.

Done means all 10 checks, 2 notes, and your writing are turned in. About 20 minutes.
