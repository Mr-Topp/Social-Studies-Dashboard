Fix the turn-in saver

This fixes the dashboard showing wrong grades, missing students and a messy layout. The turn-in script was reading your sheet's columns in the wrong order. The new one finds each column by its name. About 2 minutes. Use your school Google account. Nothing in your sheet gets changed.

  1. Copy the new script.
  2. Open your Class Turn-ins sheet, then click Extensions → Apps Script.
    Open my Class Turn-ins sheet ↗

    Make sure the file on the left says Code.gs (the turn-in saver), not your Teacher Tools script.

  3. Paste over the old code. Click inside the code, then press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+S.
  4. Publish it. Deploy (top right) → Manage deployments → pencil ✏️ → Version: New version → Deploy.

    Don't click "New deployment." That makes a new address, and students' work wouldn't reach your sheet.

  5. Check that it worked.
