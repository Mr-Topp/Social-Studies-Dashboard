    Word help (tap to open)
    • Imperialism = a strong country taking control of weaker lands and people
    • Yellow journalism = shocking, exaggerated news to sell papers
    • Annex = add land to a country
    • Ceded = handed over
    • Possessions = lands a country owns or controls
    • Inflamed = stirred up, angry
    • 1953 comic = made by the U.S. government to tell America's story in other countries. It shows one point of view.
