HOW TO PLAY

Tent or Booth?

⛺ The Ideology Tent is for beliefs: ideas about what government should do, and where those ideas come from.

🏛️ The Party Booth is for organized groups: members, leaders, money, candidates, and elections.

  1. Sort Rush: send each fairgoer's card to the tent or the booth. Fast + right = more corn.
  2. Same Ideas, Different Booths: figure out why people with the same ideology end up in different parties.
  3. Make It a Party: turn a group with shared ideas into a real party.

You can also use the ← and → arrow keys in Sort Rush. Fairgoers are made up. Iowa facts are real.

Today's goal: tell the difference between an ideology (beliefs) and a party (a team that runs candidates). SS.9-12.Gov.12
