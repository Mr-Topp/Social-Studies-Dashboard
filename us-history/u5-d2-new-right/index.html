HOW TO PLAY

Decision Desk: The New Right

The New Right was a conservative movement in the 1970s and 1980s. It helped elect Ronald Reagan.

  1. Call It: which New Right goal is each news card about? 💵 Cut taxes, 🏛️ Smaller government, ⛪ Traditional values, or 🛡️ Fight communism. Keys 1–4 work too.
  2. Two Views: a real supporter and a real critic talk about the same goal. Read their words and make the call.
  3. Write: 2 short answers, then turn in.

Wrong answer? You get a hint and try again.

Everyone in this game is real. Quotes are their real words. "Plain words" lines are our short versions.
