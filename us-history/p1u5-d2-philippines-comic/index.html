The Skipped War

1898–1914The 1953 comic jumps from 1898 straight to 1946 on the Philippines. Find the war it skipped, then check how America got the Panama Canal. 2 pages · 2 checks · 2 short answers. About 20 minutes.

Today's goal: Explain how Filipinos fought back, and how Americans argued over whether a democracy should rule a colony.
