HOW TO PLAY

Decision Desk

💭 An ideology is a set of BELIEFS about what government should do.
🏛️ A party is an organized GROUP that runs candidates and tries to win elections.

  1. Call It: is each card a liberal belief, a conservative belief, or a party action? Get 16 of 20 to pass. Keys: ← liberal, → conservative, ↓ party.
  2. Where Do They Stand? Read a voter's 3 beliefs. Are they liberal, conservative, moderate, or libertarian? Which party would they most likely vote for?
  3. Same Belief, Different Party: two people agree, but they're in different parties. Figure out why.

Liberal (usually Democrats): more government help, higher taxes on the rich, more rules, change.
Conservative (usually Republicans): smaller government, lower taxes, fewer rules, tradition.
Moderate: a mix. Libertarian: less government on everything.

Voters in this game are made up. Real facts are labeled "Real life." SS.9-12.Gov.12
