Tent or Booth?

⛺ The Ideology Tent is for BELIEFS: opinions about what the government should do.

🏛️ The Party Booth is for organized groups: members, leaders, money, candidates, and elections.

  1. Sort Rush: is it a BELIEF (tent) or a TEAM (booth)? Fast + right = more corn.
  2. Same Belief, Different Party: two people agree, but they're in different parties. Figure out why.
  3. Start a Party: what does your crew need to become a real party?

You can also use the ← and → arrow keys in Sort Rush. Fairgoers are made up. Iowa facts are real.
