    How it works: read the cartoon on the left → get the check right → keep going. Done = 5 green parts, then turn in at the bottom.

    Every cartoon was printed in a real newspaper. Most are by Herblock, who drew for the Washington Post for 55 years. A cartoon is an opinion, not a news report. Read it like a detective: labels first, then the joke.

    Word help (tap to open)
    • Conservative = wants lower taxes, smaller government, traditional values
    • Liberal = wants government to do more to help people
    • Right-wing = strongly conservative
    • G.O.P. = "Grand Old Party," a nickname for the Republicans
    • Cover-up = hiding the truth about a wrong
    • Corruption = using power to cheat or break rules
    • Resign = quit a job
    • Inflation = prices keep going up
    • Unemployment = people who want jobs can't find them
    • Stagflation = inflation + unemployment at the same time
