Update your Teacher Tools

This makes the student home page load faster, even the first time on a Chromebook. It keeps everything from before: same videos locked or unlocked, the M (missing) button, hand grades for online work, and name matching on students' to-do lists.

  1. Copy the new script.
  2. Open your Teacher Tools script.
    Open my Teacher Tools script ↗
  3. Paste over the old code. Click inside the code, then press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+S.
  4. Publish it. Deploy (top right) → Manage deployments → pencil ✏️ → Version: New version → Deploy.

    Don't click "New deployment." That makes a new address, and your site wouldn't find it.

  5. Check that it worked.
